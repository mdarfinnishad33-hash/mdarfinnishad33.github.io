Featured Projects

My Work

Practical English Mastery Program

A structured roadmap that covers grammar, vocabulary, speaking, listening, reading, and writing in one place. Each skill is taught in a way that supports the others, so learners move from knowing English to actually using it.

Problem Solved: Many students know grammar but struggle to communicate confidently. This program bridges that gap through practical learning.
Best for: learners who understand English but struggle to communicate confidently.

90-Day Spoken English Challenge

A speaking improvement program built around a daily speaking task and a weekly review, with real-life conversation practice. The daily rhythm is the point: it replaces occasional effort with a lasting habit.

Problem Solved: Helps learners overcome hesitation and develop consistent speaking habits.
Best for: learners who hesitate to speak or keep starting and stopping.

Grammar Without Memorization

Grammar taught through logic, patterns, and everyday examples. Each topic follows the same flow: a simple rule, the pattern behind it, real examples, and practice.

Problem Solved: Makes grammar easier to understand and remember.
Best for: students who find grammar confusing or boring.

Daily Vocabulary Builder

A daily series of short lessons on practical words, idioms, collocations, and phrasal verbs, each with example sentences. The focus is on words learners will actually use.

Problem Solved: Enables learners to speak and write more naturally.
Best for: learners who want to sound more natural in speaking and writing.

English Confidence Bootcamp

A short, intensive course on pronunciation, fluency, and public speaking. It is built to reduce the fear of speaking through guided practice in a supportive setting.

Problem Solved: Reduces fear of speaking English and builds communication confidence.
Best for: learners who freeze when speaking or need to present in English.
