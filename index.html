FAQ

Questions You Might Have

Who are your programs for?

Most of my programs are for beginner to intermediate learners who want practical English for speaking, writing, and everyday communication. IELTS War is designed for advanced learners aiming for a high band score.

Yes. That is exactly what my programs focus on: moving from knowing English to using it, through daily speaking practice and confidence-building.

Do you prepare students for IELTS?

Yes. I offer structured preparation across Speaking, Writing, Reading, and Listening, backed by my own Band 8 result.

Yes. Every learner gets a plan based on their strengths, weaknesses, and goals.
